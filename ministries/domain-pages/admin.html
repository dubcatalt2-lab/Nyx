Your domains

Connect your domains

  1. Create an A record for each domain pointing to your VPS. Keep your existing nameservers.
  2. Use the generated Caddy configuration when setting up this service on the VPS. It includes only domains you added.
  3. Check that HTTPS and the lessons work before requesting a category review.

Adding a domain here does not change DNS, install certificates, or request a review.

Category reviews

Lightspeed retired its public Archive lookup. Category checks and review requests now use Access Checker or AccessScan through the Filter Admin Portal and require access from a Lightspeed administrator.

If you do not have that access, ask your school's IT team to check the public domain and request a review if its category is inaccurate. This dashboard cannot submit a request for you.

Access Checker instructions

Lightspeed decides the category. A category change does not guarantee access under a school's policy.
